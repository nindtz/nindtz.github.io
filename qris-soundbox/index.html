Pasang / perbarui firmwareInstall / update firmware

  1. Beli firmware untuk model Anda di toko lynk.id kami, lalu unduh file .bin-nya. Buy the firmware for your model from our lynk.id store and download the .bin file.
  2. Buka halaman ini di Chrome atau Edge di komputer (Windows, macOS, Linux, atau Chromebook). Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux, or Chromebook).
  3. Sambungkan soundbox ke komputer dengan kabel USB (USB-C atau micro-USB, sesuai perangkat) yang mendukung data. Connect the soundbox to the computer with a USB cable (USB-C or micro-USB, whichever the device has) that supports data.
  4. Pilih file firmware di bawah. Halaman akan memberi tahu perangkat yang harus disambungkan (soundbox 3.97", Mini 1.54", atau hardware custom Anda). Centang konfirmasi, klik Pasang, lalu pilih port perangkat. Choose the firmware file below. The page tells you which device to connect (the 3.97" soundbox, the Mini 1.54", or your custom hardware). Tick the confirmation, click Install, then pick the device's port.
  5. Jika port tidak muncul atau gagal terhubung: tahan tombol BOOT, tekan dan lepas RESET (atau cabut-pasang kabel), lalu lepas BOOT dan coba lagi. If the port doesn't show up or won't connect: hold BOOT, press and release RESET (or replug the cable), release BOOT, and try again.
