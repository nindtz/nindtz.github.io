Pasang / perbarui firmwareInstall / update firmware

  1. Buka halaman ini di Chrome atau Edge di komputer (Windows, macOS, Linux, atau Chromebook). Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux, or Chromebook).
  2. Sambungkan soundbox ke komputer dengan kabel USB-C yang mendukung data. Connect the soundbox to the computer with a USB-C cable that supports data.
  3. Pilih model Anda di bawah, klik Pasang, lalu pilih port soundbox. Pick your model below, click Install, then choose the soundbox's port.
  4. Jika port tidak muncul atau gagal terhubung: tahan tombol BOOT, tekan dan lepas RESET (atau cabut-pasang kabel), lalu lepas BOOT dan coba lagi. If the port doesn't show up or won't connect: hold BOOT, press and release RESET (or replug the cable), release BOOT, and try again.
