Reviewer guide: QRIS Soundbox

Every feature can be tried on a phone alone: without a soundbox connected, the phone announces payments itself. Only Branches needs a login, and demo accounts are provided for it.

1. Core features (no login)

Announcing payments from payment notifications.

  1. Under Merchant QRIS, load the demo QRIS from section 4 (paste the text, or pick the downloaded image with Gallery) and enter a PIN, e.g. 123456.
  2. Under Payment notifications, tap Allow notification access and turn it on for QRIS Soundbox.
  3. Tap Add messaging app and pick any chat app. From another phone, send a message such as Rp 25.000 to this phone: the app announces it.
  4. Or, without another phone: under Test the box, enter an amount and tap Send.
  5. Start bridge keeps it running in the background. Customer screen shows the QRIS and each payment to customers.

2. Branches with two phones (demo accounts)

One QRIS, several soundboxes: the owner's phone forwards each payment to branch phones. Normally this runs over the merchant's Telegram account; demo numbers (+999…) log in to our demo server instead, and have Premium unlocked.

  1. Phone A: load the demo QRIS and a PIN (section 1, step 1). Under Branches, keep Owner phone, tap Link Telegram and log in with +9990001.
  2. Phone A: tap Add branch. A pairing QR appears (the share icon sends it as a link).
  3. Phone B: under Branches, choose Branch phone, tap Scan owner's QR, confirm Pair, then Link Telegram with +9990002 and tap Start bridge.
  4. Phone A: under Test the box, enter an amount and tap Send. Phone B announces it.

3. Branches with one phone

  1. Under Branches, choose Branch phone.
  2. On the same phone, open demo.nindita.id/demo and tap Pair with the demo owner, then Pair.
  3. Tap Link Telegram and log in with +9990001. The branch shows as connected to the demo owner.

Notes

  • No soundbox is needed. With one, it's chosen with Choose box (Bluetooth) and announces payments instead of the phone.
  • Demo accounts only exist on our demo server: they never reach Telegram or real users, and real accounts never use the demo server.
  • Premium (more than one branch) is a one-time in-app purchase; it's unlocked for demo accounts.
